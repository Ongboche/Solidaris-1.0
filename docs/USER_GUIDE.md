# SOLIDARIS user guide

SOLIDARIS helps a team build a **solidarity profile** of a health programme, project, policy, portfolio or investment. The profile has nine domains in three dimensions:
- **WHAT**: orientation
- **HOW**: process
- **TO WHAT END**: outcomes

Each domain is rated on its own, from 1 to 5, with evidence. Domains are **never added up, averaged or ranked**.

Site: https://ongboche.github.io/Solidaris-1.0/

---

## 1. Getting started

1. **Create an account.** Click **Create account**, fill in your details, and accept the data-protection notice. Email notifications are optional. Then confirm your email using the link we send you (check your spam folder).
2. **Everyone starts as a member.** You join a project when its Principal Investigator (PI) invites you, and the invitation sets your role on that project.
3. **Your home page** shows **What to do next**: one card per task, each with one button. Below that is your project list.
4. **Inactivity:** you are signed out after 30 minutes without activity, to protect project data. Anything you typed is saved automatically.

**Saving:** you never need a Save button. Text saves as you type ("Saved · 3 seconds ago"). If your connection drops, SOLIDARIS keeps your text on your device and retries.

## 2. Roles

| Role | What you do |
|---|---|
| **PI** | Sets up the project, invites the team, records most gate decisions, approves the profile and the report narrative |
| **Assessor** | Adds evidence and rates the nine domains **independently**. Nobody, including the PI, sees your ratings until gate G3 |
| **Reviewer** | Checks quality: verifies G2 and G6, checks the integrity review and decides G4, verifies the consensus |
| **Community participant** | Represents affected communities in deliberation; sees the validated profile |
| **External expert** | Gives specialist advice and joins deliberation |
| **KT lead** | Takes the profile to decision-makers, records the response and decides G7 |
| **Observer** | Read-only; sees the profile and reports after G6 |

## 3. The journey: gates G0 to G8

Each project page shows a bar from **G0** to **G8**. A project moves to the next stage **only** when the gate owner records a decision.

- **What the gate screen does:**
  - Checks what it can automatically and shows the evidence behind each check, for example "Missing: the decision window".
  - Asks you to answer the remaining items Yes / Partial / No / N/A. N/A needs a note.
  - Suggests a decision and explains why. The decision itself is always yours.
- **The four possible decisions:**
  - **Go**: move on.
  - **Conditional go**: move on, with conditions. They become follow-up actions, checked at the next gate.
  - **Hold**: stay at this stage until problems are fixed. A reason is required.
  - **Stop / redirect**: close the project. A reason is required.
- **Deciding above the suggestion** (for example Go when the suggestion is Hold) needs a written justification, and it is flagged in the audit log.

| Gate | Stage | Who decides |
|---|---|---|
| G0 | Scoping: the decision the profile informs, and the human-rights screen | PI |
| G1 | Context, actor map, team, conflicts of interest, community-voice plan | PI |
| G2 | Evidence for every domain (or documented gaps) | PI, after reviewer verification |
| G3 | Every assessor has submitted | PI |
| G4 | Integrity review, risk flags, solidarity type | Reviewer |
| G5 | Divergent ratings discussed; consensus or dissent recorded | PI |
| G6 | Quality assurance and member-check | PI, after reviewer verification |
| G7 | Profile delivered and the response recorded | KT lead |
| G8 | Follow-up, signals reviewed, reassessment decided | PI |

## 4. Step by step

### PI: set up (Setting up → G0 → G1)
1. **Start a new project** from your home page and choose what you're profiling.
2. **Team tab:** invite assessors (two or more are recommended), a reviewer and a community participant.
   - You get a **link** to send by email or a message app. It only works for the email address you entered.
   - If you can only have one assessor, tick **This project has only one assessor**. The profile will then be labelled *Exploratory*.
3. **Start scoping.**
4. **Scoping tab:** record the decision, the decision-maker, the decision window and the human-rights screen. Then open **Gate G0**.
5. **Context tab:** fill in financing, governance, target population and the community-voice plan, and add at least one funder, implementer, community actor and government actor. Then open **Gate G1**.

### Assessors: declare conflicts, add evidence, assess (G1 → G3)
1. **Team tab:** declare any conflicts of interest.
2. **Evidence tab:**
   - Upload files (up to 25 MB, stored privately) or add links, and tag each to the domains it informs.
   - Where evidence is missing, **log a gap** and say how it affects confidence.
3. **Assessment tab:** start your assessment. For each domain:
   - read the prompts and "What good evidence looks like";
   - in the **evidence panel**, link evidence or log a gap;
   - choose a rating (1–5), write a narrative of at least 50 characters, and pick your confidence level. Confidence is about the *strength of the evidence*.
   - Click **Mark this domain complete**. If something is missing, the button tells you what.
4. **Integrity review step:** rate all four risk flags (explain every High) and choose a primary solidarity type.
5. **Review and submit:** confirm the work is independent, then submit. It is then locked. Only the PI can reopen it, with a reason.

### Team: integrity and deliberation (G4 → G5)
- **Integrity tab:** consolidate one project-level review from the assessors' reviews. The reviewer records a quality check and decides G4.
- **Deliberation tab:**
  - Each assessor's rating appears side by side. Domains where ratings differ by more than one point are marked **Needs discussion**.
  - Post to the discussion; the PI records the consensus outcome.
  - Anyone who disagrees records **dissent**. It is kept permanently.
- **Approving the profile:** the reviewer **verifies the consensus**, then the PI **approves the profile**. After that, consensus is locked.

### Profile and report (G6)
- **Profile tab:**
  - Bars show each domain's rating on its own 1–5 scale. Paler bars mean lower confidence, and a missing bar means *Not rated*.
  - The table underneath has the same information.
  - The label shows **Exploratory**, **Awaiting validation** or **Validated**.
- **Report tab:**
  - Record member-checks with affected stakeholders.
  - Create the **auto-drafted narrative**, edit it, and (PI) approve it. It is labelled as auto-drafted until you do.
  - Export to PDF, Word, or CSV (domain ratings and the evidence register).

### Uptake, signals and learning (G7 → G8)
These three areas are proposals pending the research team's review, and may change.
- **Uptake:**
  - log the KT products delivered;
  - record the response (an action plan, or no action with a reason) and a follow-up date;
  - manage the action plan, and log each time a decision body uses the profile.
- **Due actions:** actions due within two weeks appear in *What to do next* for their owner.
- **Signals:** log observed changes per domain as **Watch** or **Act**. The PI reviews them. Signals are observations, never predictions.
- **Learning:**
  - record the follow-up review and the reassessment decision;
  - log lessons for the framework;
  - after G8, the PI can **start a reassessment**: a new, linked cycle of the same subject.

## 5. Accessibility

- SOLIDARIS works with a keyboard and screen readers.
- Press **Tab** on any page to reach **Skip to main content**.
- Every chart has a table with the same information.
- Colour is never the only signal: states are also written in words.
- It works down to 360 px wide (phones) and on slow connections.

## 6. Help

- **Text in the glossary** (dotted underline) opens a short explanation.
- **Disabled buttons** always say why they are disabled.
- **Problems:** contact the SOLIDARIS team.
